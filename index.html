CORE PRINCIPLES
🔥
Calorie deficit: Target 0.5-0.8 kg loss per week. Never go below 1800 kcal/day given high training load.
💧
Water: 3-4 liters daily minimum. On running days push to 4-5 liters with electrolytes.
😴
Sleep: 7-9 hours mandatory. Muscles grow during sleep, not during training.
📏
Measurements: Weigh every Monday morning fasted. Measure waist circumference monthly.
⚠️
Pain: Muscle fatigue = normal. Joint or tendon pain = stop and see a doctor. The difference is vital.

TRAINING TIPS
🔢
Exercise order: Always start with compound lifts (squat, bench press, deadlift, pull-up), then move to isolation (curls, flys, leg curl). Your body is strongest at the beginning of a session.
📈
Progressive overload: Add 2.5-5 kg every 2 weeks on big lifts. In calisthenics, advance to the next level once you can do 3 full, clean sets.
⚙️
Gym vs Calisthenics: On gym days: focus on weights plus 1-2 calisthenics core moves. On calisthenics days: go all in on bodyweight. The two methods complement each other perfectly.
